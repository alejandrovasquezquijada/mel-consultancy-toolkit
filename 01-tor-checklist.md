# Terms of Reference Checklist

Datimpacto · MEL Community of Practice Toolkit · Tool 1

Purpose: check a Terms of Reference against the five things it needs to do clearly before it goes out to bidders.
When to use: while drafting a ToR, or when reviewing one someone else drafted.
Time needed: 20 to 30 minutes.

## The Five Things a Strong ToR Does Clearly

| #   | Element                   | What good looks like                                                                                  | Red flag                                                                                  |
| --- | ------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | What and why now          | The object, period and geography are named, with a stated reason for commissioning now                | A generic description that could fit any program or any year                              |
| 2   | Who it's for              | Primary users are named, with the decisions they will make from the findings                          | "Stakeholders" with no names, or no stated use for the findings                           |
| 3   | Questions or deliverables | A short, prioritized list, not an exhaustive, unranked one                                            | 40 to 50 questions with no ranking, or one vague line covering everything                 |
| 4   | Boundaries                | Geography, timeframe, sample or methodological scope, and a budget ceiling are all stated             | No budget ceiling, or no indication of how many interviews, surveys or sites are expected |
| 5   | What "done" looks like    | Deliverables, review checkpoints and the approver are named, with review time built into the timeline | Deliverables listed with no review checkpoints, or a timeline with no margin for delays   |

The same five things apply to any external consultancy, not only evaluations: a MEL system design contract or a standalone data-collection engagement needs the same five elements stated clearly.

## Decision

- Ready to publish.
- Needs revision before publishing (list which of the five elements are missing or weak).
