# Active Management Kit

Datimpacto · MEL Community of Practice Toolkit · Tool 4

Purpose: apply standard project management discipline to an external consultancy while it runs.
When to use: from contract signature to final sign-off.
Time needed: 1 hour to set up, then 15 minutes a week to keep current.

The kit has four parts: a shared risk and issues log, a change request protocol, recurring check-ins and a contingency margin.

## Part 1. Shared Risk and Issues Log

One file that both sides can see and update. A risk is something that may happen; an issue is something that already did.

| ID  | Date raised | Type (Risk / Issue) | Description                                             | Likelihood (L/M/H) | Impact (L/M/H) | Owner               | Response                                         | Status | Date closed |
| --- | ----------- | ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------ | -------------- | ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ | ------ | ----------- |
| 1   |             | Risk                | Travel authorization for region 2 may be delayed        | M                  | H              | Consultant/Provider | Request permits in week 1; prepare remote option | Open   |             |
| 2   |             | Issue               | Partner B has not shared beneficiary lists for sampling |                    | H              | Commissioning team  | Escalate to partner focal point                  | Open   |             |

Rules:
- Either side can add an entry at any time.
- Review open items at every check-in.
- Anything rated High impact goes to the next milestone review (Tool 3).

## Part 2. Change Request Protocol

Any change to scope, timeline or budget goes through this form. Nothing changes until it is approved in writing.

```
CHANGE REQUEST

Request ID:
Requested by:                     Date:
Type of change:   [ ] Scope   [ ] Timeline   [ ] Budget

What changes:
Why it is needed:

Impact on priority questions or requirements (which ones, and how):
Impact on timeline (new dates):
Impact on budget (amount, and which lines):

Options considered:
  1.
  2.

Decision:  [ ] Approved   [ ] Approved with conditions   [ ] Rejected
Conditions:
Approved by:                      Date:
```

Tool 8 is an AI prompt that helps you fill in the impact fields before the decision.

## Part 3. Recurring Check-ins

Between formal milestones, hold a 30-minute check-in every one or two weeks.

Standing agenda:
1. Progress against the workplan (5 min)
2. Risk and issues log: new and open items (10 min)
3. Decisions or inputs needed from either side in the next two weeks (10 min)
4. Agreed actions, owners and dates (5 min)

Send the agreed actions in writing the same day.

## Part 4. Contingency Margin

Agree a real margin in the contract for genuine emergencies, and say what can trigger it.

- Time: for example, two to three weeks of calendar time held back from the final deadline.
- Budget: for example, around 10% of the budget held as a contingency line.
- Triggers: security incidents, access restrictions, authorization delays, logistics disruptions, public health events.
- Use: drawing on the margin still needs a change request, so there is a record.
