# Milestone Review Sheet

Datimpacto · MEL Community of Practice Toolkit · Tool 3

Purpose: treat each milestone as a decision point instead of a status update.
When to use: every time an interim product lands (inception report or design document, tools or draft components, a progress update, preliminary findings or outputs, a draft deliverable).
Time needed: 30 to 45 minutes per milestone by hand, plus the review meeting. The AI-assisted prep below runs in 5 minutes, 20 to 30 minutes to review before the meeting.

## The Question Behind Every Milestone

Does this interim product still answer our questions or requirements, on the timeline we agreed?

A milestone exists to surface "this is not going to work as designed" early enough to act on it.

## Typical Milestones and What to Decide

| Milestone                              | What to ask                                                                              | What to request from the consultant/provider                                                          |
| -------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Inception report or workplan document  | Does the design answer every priority question or requirement? Is the approach feasible? | Design matrix (question or requirement, indicator or component, source, method), sampling or workplan |
| Tools, instruments or components final | Do they cover every question or requirement in the design matrix?                        | Final tools or components, pilot or test results, ethics and consent procedures if relevant           |
| Implementation midpoint                | Are we on track for scope, sites or workstreams, and dates?                              | Aggregated progress: completed work vs target, by site or workstream                                  |
| Preliminary findings or outputs        | Is the evidence or output strong enough for each question or requirement?                | Outputs by question or requirement, with the strength of evidence or completeness stated              |
| Draft deliverable                      | Is it ready for the sign-off review?                                                     | Draft plus a response matrix for comments                                                             |

## Review Checks

| #   | Check                                                                                                                                                                                         | Status (On track / At risk / Off track) | Notes |
| --- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- | ----- |
| 1   | Each priority question or requirement is still answerable with the planned data or approach                                                                                                   |                                         |       |
| 2   | The timeline to the final deliverable still holds                                                                                                                                             |                                         |       |
| 3   | Quality signals are acceptable (for a report or study: response rates vs target, missing data, sites dropped; for a system, tool or dataset: coverage, error rates, components not yet built) |                                         |       |
| 4   | Any change to methods, sample or design is documented and justified                                                                                                                           |                                         |       |
| 5   | Open items in the risk and issues log have an owner and a response                                                                                                                            |                                         |       |
| 6   | The provider has stated what the deliverable can and cannot support                                                                                                                           |                                         |       |

## Decision

Choose one and record it in the minutes:

- Continue as planned.
- Continue with a documented adjustment (use the change request in Tool 4).
- Pause and redesign part of the engagement.

## AI-Assisted Milestone Prep

Purpose: check whether an interim product still answers your questions or requirements on the agreed timeline, and prepare the decision above for the milestone meeting.
When to use: when an inception report, progress update or preliminary findings arrive, before the meeting.

### What You Need

- The ToR questions or requirements and timeline.
- The interim product (inception report, progress report, preliminary findings or draft component).
- Aggregated quality figures if relevant: completed work vs target by site or workstream, response rates, share of missing data or unresolved items on key variables or components.

Data note: use aggregated figures and report text only. Do not paste datasets or anything at the level of individual beneficiaries.

### The Prompt

```
You are a commissioning manager preparing for a milestone review of an
external consultancy. Your job is to answer one question: does this interim
product still answer our questions or requirements, on the timeline we agreed?

Milestone: [e.g. inception report / implementation midpoint / preliminary findings]

Questions or requirements and agreed timeline:
[PASTE QUESTIONS OR REQUIREMENTS AND TIMELINE]

Aggregated quality figures (if available):
[PASTE TABLE, e.g. site or workstream | target | achieved | response rate | missing data or open items %]

Task:
1. For each priority question or requirement, assess whether the interim
   product shows a credible path to meeting it. Rate: On track / At risk /
   Off track, and explain in one or two sentences.
2. Compare progress with the agreed timeline. Flag any slippage and its likely
   effect on the final deliverable date.
3. Review the quality figures. Flag sites, workstreams or questions where low
   response, missing data or unresolved items weaken the evidence or output.
4. State plainly what the product is likely to support and what it is not,
   question by question or requirement by requirement.
5. List any changes to methods, sample or design that appear in the product
   but were not formally agreed.
6. Propose two or three decision options for the meeting (for example:
   continue as planned; continue with a specific adjustment; redesign part of
   the engagement), with the trade-off of each.
7. List the questions the commissioning team should ask the consultant/provider.

Output format:
A. Table: Question or requirement | Status | Reason | What the evidence can support
B. Timeline assessment
C. Quality flags
D. Unagreed changes
E. Decision options with trade-offs
F. Questions for the provider

Rules:
- Reference the section or page of the interim product for each point.
- If information needed for an assessment is missing, say so and list it under F.

Interim product:
[PASTE TEXT HERE]
```

### How to Read the Output

- Focus on At risk and Off track rows. These are the reason to hold the meeting.
- Section D matters: changes that slipped in without a change request can become disputes at sign-off.
- The decision options are a starting point. Your team knows the donor, the partners and the field.

### What Stays Human

The decision at the milestone: continue, adjust or redesign, and any change request that follows.
