# Managing an External Consultancy: One-Page Checklist

Datimpacto · MEL Community of Practice Toolkit · Tool 6

What has to be in place before a final deliverable lands.

## 1. A Clear Terms of Reference

- [ ] Object, period, geography and the reason for commissioning now are stated
- [ ] Primary users and the decisions they will make are named
- [ ] Priority questions or requirements are few and ranked
- [ ] Scope boundaries and a budget ceiling are set
- [ ] Deliverables, review checkpoints and the approver are defined
- [ ] The timeline adds up, with room for delays

## 2. A Defensible Selection Process

- [ ] Pass/fail criteria are published before the call
- [ ] Weighted criteria and their weights are published
- [ ] At least two people score each proposal independently
- [ ] AI triage, if used, stops at a first pass
- [ ] The final decision and its reason are written down

## 3. Milestones Treated as Decision Points

- [ ] Each milestone has a decision question attached
- [ ] The inception report or design document includes a design matrix and sampling or workplan
- [ ] Quality signals are reviewed at each milestone
- [ ] Each review ends with a recorded decision: continue, adjust or redesign

## 4. Active Management While the Work Runs

- [ ] A shared risk and issues log is in use by both sides
- [ ] Changes go through a written change request
- [ ] Check-ins happen every one or two weeks between milestones
- [ ] A contingency margin for time and budget is agreed in the contract

## 5. A Seven-Point Quality Sign-off

- [ ] It answers what was asked
- [ ] The product is trustworthy and limitations are stated
- [ ] The underlying work is sound
- [ ] The findings or outputs are credible
- [ ] Conclusions follow from the evidence
- [ ] The deliverable is usable (who, what and when for each recommendation)
- [ ] It is readable by the team that has to run with it
